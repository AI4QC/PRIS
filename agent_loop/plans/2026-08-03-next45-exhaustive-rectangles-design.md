# NEXT45 exhaustive rectangular guard design

NEXT44's ranking reaches high precision only at roughly 3--5% rejection and loses precision before the required 10% savings. NEXT45 tests a distinct finite hypothesis without adding descriptors: a chemically conditional rejection region may exist even when one feature's marginal AUC is modest.

The catalogue exhaustively combines every coverage-qualified NEXT43/NEXT44 feature pair, both high- and low-risk directions, and a fixed discovery-quantile tail grid. Each law is a two-condition conjunction. Conditions are evaluated with exact bitsets; missing values KEEP. Discovery labels alone determine quantiles and select one rectangle. Internal validation remains untouched until selection.

The coverage, protected-recall, rejection-precision, and savings gates remain unchanged. No learned classifier, DFT execution feature, model potential, or relaxation is introduced. A passing internal rectangle would still require frozen unseen-shard confirmation.
