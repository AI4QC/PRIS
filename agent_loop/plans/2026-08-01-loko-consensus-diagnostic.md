# Post-hoc LOKO consensus diagnostic

This is a descriptive, post-hoc analysis performed after the complete
`np-next-20260801e` LOKO result was visible.  It is not a new validation split,
does not reset any gate, and cannot turn the failed soft-margin search into a
confirmed law.

For each semantic rule identity (target feature, family, side, numerical
thresholds, and optional guard), count at most one occurrence per held-kind
refit.  Report every identity appearing in at least two of five refits.  Define
the narrow consensus candidate deterministically as the identity or identities
with the largest recurrence count; ties are retained together.  Append that
fixed consensus set to the full-fit existing-loop baseline without refitting and
report discovery and adaptively reused calibration deltas for satisfaction,
pooled rejection, minimum-kind rejection, every perturbation kind, and worst
shared anion.

The result is evidence of recurrence and mechanism locality only.  No lockbox
is read, no threshold is tuned, and no canonical script, report, or manuscript
is modified.
