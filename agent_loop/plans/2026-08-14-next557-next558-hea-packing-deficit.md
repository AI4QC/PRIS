## NEXT557 frozen law

`primitive_covalent_packing_fraction__risk_low` with no transformation,
coefficient, threshold fit, or additional feature.  Operational screening
rejects the highest-risk 15% of a candidate batch, breaking ties by FID.

initial covalent-sphere packing capacity raises the risk of either extreme
DFT energy-above-hull or a large DFT structural response.

NEXT557 may freeze this law only after verifying the exact NEXT555 univariate
metrics above, the absence of an eligible pair, and the still-sealed validation
flags.  It opens no new endpoint.
