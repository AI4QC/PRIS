# NEXT407 bibliographic erratum

The frozen NEXT407 design contains a transcription error in the DOI of the
Langer--Kohlmann source.  The authoritative IUCr article page gives
`10.1107/S2052520626001794`, not `10.1107/S2052520626002298`:

https://journals.iucr.org/b/issues/2026/02/00/ra5166/

This erratum corrects bibliography only.  It does not alter the already frozen
formula, feature direction, graph, gates, executable code, probe result, or
their recorded SHA-256 identities.
