# NEXT78 electrostatic-augmented finite tail search

## Scope and firewall

Repeat the finite NEXT72 anchor-plus-correction catalogue with the new NEXT77
label-free classical electrostatic features added alongside NEXT65, NEXT70, and
NEXT75.  The opened internal-validation endpoint and its predictions are
forbidden inputs.  Search uses robust discovery only; internal replication
remains physically unopened.

## Frozen catalogue

Anchor the exact sealed NEXT67 three terms with threshold removed.  Candidate
guards are the frozen NEXT65 physics couplings, NEXT70 metal--donor
bond-valence features, NEXT75 metal--ligand rigidity features, and all 27
NEXT77 analytic electrostatic columns.  Require at least 95% finite
discovery-domain coverage and nonzero IQR.  Enumerate anchor alone, every
one-guard sign with weight 0.125, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, or 8, then every pair
among the leading 24 one-guard identities with independent signs and weights
0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, or 4.  Evaluate the unchanged 0.02--0.30 thresholds and the
unchanged seven scientific gates, missing=KEEP, strata, rank, and tie break.

Because the exact-threshold NEXT73 rule passed discovery by a narrow precision
margin and then failed one-shot internal validation, a candidate may advance
to the remaining replication lockbox only when it passes all original gates
and its discovery reject-precision Wilson lower bound is at least 0.75.  This
safety margin does not weaken any original gate.  No post-hoc exact threshold
refinement is allowed in NEXT78.

## Boundary

The output remains a maximum five-term raw-x0 analytic sum and fixed threshold.
The electrostatic descriptors are deterministic point-charge Ewald, analytic
steric-balance, and charge-spectrum calculations on the unchanged input cell;
they are not DFT or learned energy/force/stress proxies.  No DFT calculation or
value, relaxed geometry, opened validation result, proxy potential, physical
relaxation, or same-composition alternative is an input.
