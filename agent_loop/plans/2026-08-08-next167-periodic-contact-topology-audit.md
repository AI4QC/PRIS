cross-source, threshold-local structural signal absent from the existing
scalar mechanism families.

**Architecture:** Join the frozen NEXT166 discovery features to the exact
NEXT164 closest-candidate score and evaluate a finite, direction-frozen

**Tech stack:** Python, NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn ROC AUC, parquet, pytest.

## Eligibility and termination

Each feature must have at least 90% finite support in both sources' full
extremes. Eligibility then requires:

- SCIGEN repair-shell worst-fold AUC `>= 0.55`, all five folds evaluable;
- WyFormer repair-shell pooled AUC `>= 0.55`;
- both full-source pooled AUCs `>= 0.50`.

Rank eligible hypotheses by the minimum of those four AUCs, their mean, then
hypothesis name. If none is eligible, terminate periodic topology. If one or
more are eligible, only the top-ranked hypothesis may enter a separately
frozen NEXT168 law search. NEXT167 itself cannot claim a replacement or open
validation.
