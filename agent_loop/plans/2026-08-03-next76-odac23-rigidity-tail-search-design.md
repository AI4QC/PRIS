## Scope and firewall

Repeat the finite NEXT72 anchor-plus-correction catalogue with the new NEXT75
label-free rigidity features added.  The opened internal-validation endpoint
and its predictions are forbidden inputs.  Search uses robust discovery only;
internal replication remains physically unopened.

## Frozen catalogue

Anchor the exact sealed NEXT67 three terms with threshold removed.  Candidate
guards are NEXT65, NEXT70, and NEXT75 feature columns.  Require at least 95%
finite discovery-domain coverage and nonzero IQR.  Enumerate anchor alone,
every one-guard sign with weight 0.125, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, or 8, then every
pair among the leading 24 one-guard identities with independent signs and
weights 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, or 4.  Evaluate unchanged 0.02--0.30 thresholds and
the unchanged seven scientific gates, missing=KEEP, strata, rank, and tie
break.

Because NEXT73 barely crossed the discovery precision gate and failed its
one-shot validation, a candidate may advance to the remaining replication
lockbox only when it passes all original gates and its discovery
reject-precision Wilson lower bound is at least 0.75.  This safety margin does
not weaken any original gate.  No exact post-hoc threshold refinement is
allowed in NEXT76.

## Boundary

The output remains a maximum five-term raw-x0 analytic sum and fixed threshold.
No DFT calculation/value, relaxed geometry, opened validation result,
energy/force/stress model, proxy potential, physical relaxation, or
same-composition alternative is an input.
