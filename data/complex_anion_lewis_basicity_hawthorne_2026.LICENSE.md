# Complex-anion Lewis-basicity table attribution

The adjacent CSV transcribes the eleven non-hydroxylated simple complex-anion
rows from Appendix 4 of Frank C. Hawthorne, “Crystal chemistry: New rules for
the 21st century,” *Mineralogical Magazine* (2026), DOI
10.1180/mgm.2026.10215. The article is distributed under CC BY 4.0.

Hydroxylated rows are intentionally excluded because raw element identities do
not uniquely assign protons to individual ligand oxygen sites. The numerical
Lewis-basicity values are preserved as printed.
