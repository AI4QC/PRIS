# Characteristic Lewis acidity table attribution

The adjacent CSV transcribes the characteristic coordination numbers and
characteristic Lewis acidities printed in Appendix 3 of:

Frank C. Hawthorne, “Crystal chemistry: New rules for the 21st century,”
*Mineralogical Magazine* (2026), DOI: 10.1180/mgm.2026.10215.

The article is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International licence (CC BY 4.0). The H+ row is intentionally omitted because
the source prints two starred characteristic acidity values rather than one
unique lookup value. All other numerical values are transcribed as printed;
they have not been recomputed from the characteristic coordination number.
